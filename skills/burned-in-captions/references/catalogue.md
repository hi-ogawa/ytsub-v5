# Catalogue of Caption Patterns

Each pattern pairs a feature signature with a reading rule. The column names are in [Features](../SKILL.md#features). With regions set, read `kept`, `gone`, and `new` as the region's columns, and a cluster as a region. Patterns marked unconfirmed haven't turned up in a video yet.

| Pattern                                                      | Feature signature                                                                                                                                                                  | Reading rule                                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Swap                                                         | `kept` drops, `gone` and `new` both high                                                                                                                                           | one late frame per side                                                                           |
| Swap in the same spot and size                               | `gone` and `new` spike far above the steps around them while `scene` stays low. `kept` may drop clearly or barely, depending on how much of the new text lands on the old letters. | read both sides of the spike                                                                      |
| Reveal or typing                                             | `new` grows while `gone` stays near zero and `kept` stays high                                                                                                                     | read the last frame of the run, and merge the partial line into the fuller one                    |
| Reveal that ends in a separate line                          | a reveal one step before a swap, adding words that read as another speaker                                                                                                         | keep as one caption, but say so, since it may be two lines                                        |
| Crossfade                                                    | `kept_1s` and `kept_2s` fall slowly while `kept` stays high, with no swap in the 1–2 s before                                                                                      | read after the fade settles                                                                       |
| Fade in or out                                               | `letters` ramps up or down over 1–2 steps                                                                                                                                          | skip the ramp frames. A read that lands on one comes back noted, and merges with the steady read. |
| Side caption with the main one                               | two regions or clusters with independent change times                                                                                                                              | read when either changes, and keep the side caption as its own caption                            |
| Two-line side caption in stacked boxes                       | two dark boxes one above the other in a side zone                                                                                                                                  | one caption, with the lines joined by a space                                                     |
| Commentary that stays while lines change                     | one cluster holds while another swaps                                                                                                                                              | read each swap, and take the held text once                                                       |
| Caption holds across a camera cut                            | `scene` spikes, but the region's `kept` stays high, or the clusters stay put                                                                                                       | no new read                                                                                       |
| Very short caption                                           | a run of steps under 0.5 s between changes                                                                                                                                         | read it anyway, since it may be speech                                                            |
| Scenery as text                                              | whole-frame `letters` stays in the thousands with no caption, `kept` drops at every camera cut, and clusters move with the scene                                                   | use regions                                                                                       |
| Logo on screen most of the video                             | a box in `static.json`                                                                                                                                                             | no read, since it is already dropped                                                              |
| Sponsor banner in the caption area                           | several lines of text in the bottom area during an ad, often with ✨ or ※, held for several seconds                                                                                | read it once per change as `other`. The narration under it has no `speech` caption.               |
| Typed text card                                              | a card with a title, then body text that grows letter by letter                                                                                                                    | `other`, read at the end of the typing                                                            |
| Stretch with speech but no caption                           | ASR lines with no caption in time, and every region below its empty level                                                                                                          | read every other step to confirm, then leave it empty                                             |
| Logo or banner on screen for part of the video (unconfirmed) | a cluster holds its position and size across many caption changes, but is on screen in less than half the frames                                                                   | read it once, then ignore it for that stretch                                                     |
| Caption overlapping a dropped overlay (unconfirmed)          | `in_static` above its usual level, next to a caption cluster                                                                                                                       | read the frame anyway, since the masks miss the part under the overlay                            |
| Section title held for minutes (unconfirmed)                 | cluster in a corner that changes rarely                                                                                                                                            | read it once per change as `other`                                                                |

## Notes and How to Settle Them

Readers describe what they see in a free-form `note`, and the model pass in [Assemble and correct](../SKILL.md#5-assemble-and-correct) decides what to do with each noted read. These cases are examples of that judgment, not a fixed list, so a new case needs no new rule. They come from one 21-minute variety episode, `2nP5PNX8Adg`, where large editorial text often fades in after a camera cut, and dense cards carry small handwriting.

| Case                                                                                                                                       | A note that flags it                                  | Decision                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Large text fading in after a cut, read as `예[?] 사투리[?] 안 쓰게 된 이유` next to the steady `예주가 사투리를 안 쓰게 된 이유` at 612.25 | faint, still fading in, same caption as the next tile | merge into the steady read, which gives the caption the fade's begin       |
| The same kind of fade at 775.5, and on silent editorial inserts at 391 and 736                                                             | faint, fading in                                      | merge into the steady read next to it                                      |
| An end card unfolding at 1246, giving partial reads                                                                                        | end card still unfolding                              | keep the read after it settles, and merge the partial reads into it        |
| Review cards at 615.75, 821.25, and 929, and a letter card at 55.75, seen only in full-frame tiles                                         | small handwriting, can't read at this size            | reread from a 2× crop of the card area                                     |
| A clip of an earlier video, with its own burned-in lyrics, at 1093.5                                                                       | clip from another video with its own lyrics           | decide whether the clip belongs in the track. Here it doesn't, so drop it. |
| Name tags (주니), (이지), and (예주) at 118–121, when the members first appear                                                             | name tags on each member                              | keep each one as its own `other` caption                                   |
| The members singing live at 1044.5–1061.25, with the lyrics captioned                                                                      | `omitted: "lyrics"` rather than a note                | keep as one omitted caption with its run times, and list it in the report  |
